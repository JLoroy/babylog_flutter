Delete your Babylog account and data

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Babylog users can request deletion of their account and associated Babylog data from this page without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion by email

Email privacy@lenacho.be with the subject "Babylog account deletion request" and include the email address used for your Babylog account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing deletion.

Delete inside the app

You can also delete your account inside the Babylog app from Settings.

What deletion covers

When your account is deleted, Babylog deletes your Firebase Auth account, your Babylog user profile, events for your current assistant or timeline, your membership in the assistant or timeline, and the locally stored OpenAI key for that assistant. If no users remain in the assistant, the assistant document is deleted.

Data processed by service providers, including OpenAI API processing logs, may be retained according to those providers' policies and the relevant API account settings. Some records may also be retained if required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or operational reasons.

Privacy policy

Read the Babylog Privacy Policy for more information about how Babylog handles data.
